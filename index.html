About Us

The Northern Uganda Filmmakers Association

NUFMA is a permanent home for Northern Uganda's film industry — training, connecting, and championing local filmmakers year-round. The Northern Uganda Film Awards (NUFA) is our flagship program, honoring the best in storytelling, cinematography, and artistic expression.
